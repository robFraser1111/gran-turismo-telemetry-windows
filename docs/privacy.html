SlickDash privacy policy

Effective 2 September 2026. Operator: Rob Fraser (Australia), publishing as SlickDash. Not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Polyphony Digital.

What SlickDash is

SlickDash is a Windows app that shows live racing telemetry from Gran Turismo 7 on a PlayStation 5 on your local network. It does not require an account.

Data that stays on your PC

View preference and the last PlayStation 5 address are stored in a local settings.json file on your computer. Lap times and fuel figures for the current session stay in memory and are discarded when you quit. We do not operate a SlickDash cloud or user database.

Local network

On the same LAN, SlickDash sends UDP heartbeats and receives telemetry packets from a PS5 running Gran Turismo 7. That traffic does not go through our servers.

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes, it may send a diagnostic report to Sentry (a third-party error service we use) so we can fix bugs. Those reports can include the exception, app version, OS version, and network address information. We strip the machine name before send. Crash reports are not used for advertising. Race telemetry packets are not uploaded.

What we do not do

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no selling personal information, no tracking you across other apps or sites.

Children

SlickDash is a racing dashboard, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/robFraser1111/gran-turismo-telemetry-windows/issues.
