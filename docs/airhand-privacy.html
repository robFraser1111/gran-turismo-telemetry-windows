AirHand privacy policy

Effective 9 September 2026. Operator: Rob Fraser (Australia), publishing as AirHand. Not affiliated with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd., Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Air-Conditioning Europe, Smart M-Air, or related trademarks.

What AirHand is

AirHand is a local Wi‑Fi remote for compatible Mitsubishi Heavy Industries WF‑RAC / Smart M‑Air style indoor units. It runs as a native app on your phone. It does not require a Mitsubishi account and does not use the manufacturer’s cloud for day‑to‑day control.

Data that stays on your device

Preferences such as light/dark mode, temperature display unit, room names, room icons, and the list of registered unit addresses are stored locally in the app (Android DataStore / iOS preferences). We do not operate an AirHand cloud account system or user database.

Local network

On the same Wi‑Fi / LAN, AirHand talks to your units over HTTP on port 51443 (the module’s local “beaver” API), including cleartext where the firmware requires it. That traffic stays on your network. We do not proxy those commands through our servers.

What we do not do

No AirHand accounts, no ads, no selling personal information, no tracking you across other apps or sites. The app does not use advertising IDs. Current Play builds do not ship third‑party crash analytics; if that changes later, this page will be updated before those builds go out.

Children

AirHand is a home climate remote, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on airhand-android or airhand-ios.
